Privacy Policy

Effective date: September 23, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how RELXTAN handles information in connection with its website, internal tools, and integrations with the Amazon Ads API.

1. Information We May Process

When using our internal Amazon Ads API tools, we may process advertising account data made available through Amazon, including campaign information, ad groups, keywords, targeting settings, bids, budgets, performance metrics, and reporting data.

2. How We Use Information

We use information only for legitimate internal business purposes, including:

  • Managing and analyzing Amazon advertising campaigns;
  • Generating advertising performance reports;
  • Reviewing keywords, bids, budgets, targeting, and campaign structure;
  • Improving internal advertising workflows and automation;
  • Maintaining the security and reliability of our tools.

3. Amazon Ads API Data

Data obtained through the Amazon Ads API is used only for authorized advertising management and analysis purposes. We do not sell Amazon Ads API data or use it for unrelated purposes.

4. Data Sharing

We do not sell or rent personal information. Information may be shared only when necessary to operate authorized services, comply with legal obligations, protect rights and security, or work with service providers that support our internal systems.

5. Data Retention

We retain information only for as long as reasonably necessary for the purposes described in this Privacy Policy, or as required by applicable law and relevant platform requirements.

6. Data Security

We use reasonable technical and organizational safeguards designed to protect information against unauthorized access, disclosure, alteration, or loss. Access credentials, tokens, and other sensitive authentication information are intended to be restricted to authorized users and systems.

7. Third-Party Services

Our tools may interact with third-party services, including Amazon Ads. Those services operate under their own terms and privacy policies.

8. Your Choices and Rights

Where applicable, you may contact us regarding questions about personal information, access, correction, deletion, or other privacy-related requests.

9. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The effective date shown at the top of this page indicates the latest revision.
